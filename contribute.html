🌳 Why your photos & memories matter

The biggest gap in this research is not another census search. It is family photographs, letters, and memories — the material that shows what people looked like, where they lived, how they spoke about each other, and what mattered to them.

One shared photo can change the whole project. A wedding group might identify cousins. A prayer card might confirm a burial place. A postcard might explain why a branch of the family moved from Portarlington to Dublin, Jersey, Biberach, or New Zealand.

Even a blurry phone photo is useful. The first job is preservation; we can improve scans, crop images, and document the details afterwards.

Small clue, big impact: if you have a photo but only know “this was Granny’s side” or “taken somewhere in Dublin”, please still send it. Unknowns are part of the research, not a blocker.

📸 What we'd love to see

  • Portraits — any era, any quality
  • Wedding, christening, or first communion photos
  • Letters or postcards
  • Identity documents, including passports, ration books, or internment records
  • Audio recordings of family stories
  • Memorabilia, including medals, prayer cards, and funeral cards
  • Bridget Clarke / Thomas Greene era especially: anything from the 1870s–1970s connected to Portarlington, Dublin, Drumcondra, Inchicore, Goldenbridge Avenue, Jersey, or Biberach

🤝 What we promise

  • We'll credit you — or keep the contribution anonymous if you prefer.
  • We won't share or publish anything without your consent.
  • Originals stay with you. Scans or phone photos are enough to start.
  • If you let us scan an original, we'll send back a high-quality digitisation for your own family archive.

📨 How to share

The simplest route is email. If that is awkward, a shared folder link works just as well.

Email contribute@garrigan.me

If this mailbox is not live yet, Mark will route it before sharing the page widely.

Fallback Open a GitHub issue

Use this for non-private notes or to ask how best to share a larger file.

  • Photos: JPG or PNG, preferably original resolution.
  • Documents: PDF, JPG, or PNG. 600+ DPI is ideal if scanning.
  • Audio: MP3 or M4A.
  • Helpful notes: who, what, when, and where — even if approximate.
  • Large files: WeTransfer, Google Drive, or OneDrive links are fine.

🔒 Privacy & living people

This is a personal family research site. It is built to preserve and explain family history, not to expose private information.

Information about living people is included with the family's awareness. If you would prefer your name, a relative's name, or a detail about a living person to be redacted, just let us know and we will handle it respectfully.

📜 Documenting unknowns

Please do not wait until everything is labelled perfectly. “I have a photo but don't know who's in it” is still valuable.

Faces, uniforms, houses, studio stamps, handwriting, place names, and even the order of photos in an album can all become clues. Someone else in the family may recognise what one person cannot.

Send the uncertain things too. The research community is often strongest when several people each recognise one small detail.
