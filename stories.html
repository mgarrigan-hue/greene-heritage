Civil registers can tell us when someone was born, married, or died. They cannot tell us why a young couple moved house, what a wedding day felt like, or what kind of person someone was. This page collects the narrative material — family memories, research breakthroughs, and the moments where archive evidence and lived memory meet. If you have a story to add, please share it.
