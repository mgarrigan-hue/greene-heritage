# Phase 19 — Joseph Greene context

## Current family.json position

`family.json` already treats Joseph Greene as **not** Thomas Christopher Greene's father. That correction still holds: the 1922 Thomas Greene × Bridget Clarke civil marriage register names Thomas's father as **William Greene, Traveller**, not Joseph Greene.

Joseph remains a separate Portarlington-line Greene: confirmed father of Mary Bridget Greene (b.1874, Portarlington) from the inherited/Ancestry context, but not currently connected to William by a primary-source relationship.

## Civil-index searches run

Search artefacts are under this folder; the aggregate `results.json` combines the captured `results.json` files from each sub-search.

### Direct Joseph × Elizabeth Foran marriage search

- `ig-marriage-joseph-greene-elizabeth-foran-all-1860-1910`: no results.
- `ig-marriage-joseph-green-elizabeth-foran-all-1860-1910`: no results.

### Local Joseph marriage/death searches

- `ig-marriage-joseph-greene-laois-1860-1910`: no results.
- `ig-marriage-joseph-green(e)-offaly-1860-1910`: no results for either spelling.
- `ig-death-joseph-green(e)-offaly-1860-1910`: no results for either spelling.
- Broad all-Ireland searches returned many Joseph Green/Greene records; the first captured pages did not identify a Portarlington / Mountmellick / Lower Forest overlap. The only local-ish first-page items were not persuasive: Joseph Greene death, Athy 1883 age 70 (born c.1813; too old for the likely Joseph father of Mary Bridget b.1874), plus Joseph Green marriages in Carlow 1901 and Birr 1907 (too late / wrong spouse context).

### Child-context searches

- Birth searches for Green(e) children with parent Joseph, 1864-1890, returned no results in both all-Ireland and Laois-scoped runs.
- Focused Mary/Bridget Green(e) birth searches for Laois, 1873-1875, returned no results.
- All-Ireland Mary/Bridget Green(e) 1873-1875 searches returned broad result sets but the captured first pages did not show a Portarlington/Laois match.

## Conclusion

No Phase 19 source connects Joseph Greene to William Greene of Lower Forest / Mountmellick, and no evidence was found that Joseph was William's brother. The disambiguation therefore holds: Joseph should remain father of Mary Bridget Greene only, while Thomas Christopher Greene's proven father remains William Greene.

Pull Mary Bridget Greene's actual birth/baptism record from the source behind the inherited Ancestry claim, then use the recorded address, sponsors, and informant to test whether Joseph's household overlaps with William Green × Eliza Woods or the wider Lower Forest cluster.
