DONNELLY, Professor Dervilla M.X.: Death

Death
780
Published on the Irish Times website on 17th November 2024

DONNELLY, Dervilla M. X. (Professor) (Dartry, Dublin) (14th November 2024) peacefully after a short illness at St. James’s Hospital, predeceased by her sisters Liobháin (Meenan) and Keara (Kyra) and dear aunt of Frances, Charles and John, deeply regretted by her niece, nephews, grandnieces, grandnephews, great grandnieces, relatives and friends. Requiescat in Pace. Removal tomorrow Tuesday morning (19th November) to the Church of the Three Patrons, Rathgar arriving at 10.50 am for Funeral Mass at 11.00 am which may be viewed on the parish webcam, followed by cremation at the Victorian Chapel, Mount Jerome, Harold’s Cross.
