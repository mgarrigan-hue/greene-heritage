DONNELLY, John: Death

Death
409
Published on the Irish Times website on 24th February 2023

DONNELLY, (Glenageary, Co. Dublin and member of Blainroe Golf Club) February 22, 2023, peacefully in the kind care of the staff in St. Vincent’s Hospital. John Nugent beloved husband of the late Eileen (Cecilia) loving father of Olwyn and Richard. Sadly missed by his children and their partners Stephen and Jill, adored grandchildren Meg, Patrick and Jamie, his brother James, sisters Anne and Geraldine, brothers-in-law, sisters-in-law, extended family, neighbours and friends. Removal Monday (February 27) from Quinn’s of Glasthule to St. Joseph’s Church, Glasthule arriving for 10.000am Funeral Mass which can be viewed online at www.churchservices.tv/glasthule followed by burial in Kilternan Cemetery Park. May he Rest in Peace
