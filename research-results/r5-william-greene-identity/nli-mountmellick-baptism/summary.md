## Finding

No baptism entry was visually identified for Thomas / Thomas Christopher Green(e), child of William Green(e) and Eliza/Elizabeth Woods, in the Mountmellick baptism register pages covering Feb. 1870 to Feb. 1871.

This means Phase 19 did **not** cross-confirm the Phase 18 William Green × Eliza Woods marriage candidate via Mountmellick baptism evidence. The 1868 Mountmellick marriage remains the strongest candidate for William Greene's identity, but it should not be upgraded to confirmed on this negative NLI pass alone.
