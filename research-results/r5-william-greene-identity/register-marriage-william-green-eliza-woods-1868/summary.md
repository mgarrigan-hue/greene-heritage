## Why this matters

This is the strongest candidate yet for our William Greene (Thomas Greene's father) and would push the family tree back another generation:

- **New ancestor candidate: Thomas Green Snr**, farmer of Lower Forest, Queens County — likely father of William and grandfather of Thomas Greene Jnr (b. ~1870)
- William's age "full" (meaning 21+) at Feb 1868 fits the 1842-1844 birth window implied by the 1909/1910 death candidates aged 66-67
- Lower Forest (a townland near Mountmellick) is geographically consistent with the Portarlington Catholic parish where Thomas Greene Jnr was baptised ~July 1870
- Naming pattern fits Irish RC tradition: William would name his first son Thomas after his own father, and our Thomas Greene Jnr is named exactly that

## Caveats

- Not yet cross-confirmed against Thomas Greene Jnr's NLI Portarlington baptism record (pages 142-145, captured but not transcribed)
- Mother (Eliza Woods → "Greene née Woods") not yet attested in any other source we hold
- If William was a widower by 1909 (per cide-5027279 — see that summary), Eliza's death record would also need to be found
