## Pattern note for future research

IG.ie's "View record images" link sometimes points to the wrong file (off by a few entries, or to a different register page entirely). When using IG.ie image extracts as evidence:
1. Always cross-verify the transcribed name in the register against the IG.ie metadata table.
2. If they don't match, manually page-flip ± a few image numbers to find the real entry.
3. Do not assume the linked image matches the headline transcription.
