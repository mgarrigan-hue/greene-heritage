No William Green is on this page.

This is another **IG.ie image-link bug** (consistent with cima-3052584, cide-5096314, cide-4468003). The IG.ie transcription panel still attests that a William Green age 65 died in Mountmellick area in 1878, but no verified register image is held.

If real, an 1878 William Green age 65 was born ~1813 — this is too OLD to be our William who married 1868 (he was born ~1843-44), but **could be a generation above**: possibly Thomas Green Snr's brother, or another William in the wider family network.
